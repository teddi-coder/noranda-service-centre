Our Audi Services

Audi Logbook Servicing

Manufacturer scheduled servicing using premium oils and correct service resets. Keep your warranty intact and your Audi performing exactly as German engineers intended.

DSG Transmission Servicing

Because replacing a DSG gearbox is significantly less fun than servicing one. DSG fluid, filter replacement, inspections and software checks for smoother shifting and longer transmission life.

Diagnostics & Warning Lights

When your dashboard lights up, we find the actual problem. Advanced fault scanning, ECU diagnostics, electrical fault testing and sensor checks fast answers, no unnecessary part swapping.

Cooling System Repairs

Water pump repairs, thermostat replacement, coolant leak repairs and radiator inspections. Audi cooling systems can be dramatic in Perth's heat don't wait for an overheating bill.

Audi Engine Repairs

Timing issues, oil leaks, cooling system failures, carbon buildup, turbo issues and engine diagnostics handled properly.

Brakes & Suspension

Brake pads and rotors, suspension repairs, steering repairs and wheel alignment checks to keep your Audi feeling sharp, smooth, and safe.

Air Conditioning Repairs

AC diagnostics, regas services, compressor repairs and cooling inspections to keep your Audi comfortable year-round.

Preventative Maintenance

Because waiting until your Audi makes expensive noises is rarely the smart financial move. We help prevent major repairs before they happen.
