Why AC Systems Fail Over Time

Car air conditioning systems lose refrigerant naturally over time even without a fault. In WA's heat, your system works harder and cools less efficiently. Compressor strain, small leaks, and refrigerant degradation all contribute to reduced performance. A regas restores the refrigerant to the correct level.

If there's an underlying leak or compressor fault, we'll diagnose it and let you know exactly what's needed before any work begins.
