Frequently Asked Questions

The ECU (Engine Control Unit) is the brain of your engine. Remapping adjusts the factory settings to optimise power, torque and fuel delivery. For 4WDs used for towing, caravanning or off road driving, a remap can make a noticeable real-world difference to towing grunt, overtaking confidence and fuel economy.
Results vary by vehicle and engine, but most diesel 4WDs see meaningful gains in low-end torque — the pulling power that matters most when towing or overtaking with a caravan. We assess your specific vehicle first and give you an honest expectation before any work is done.
A Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) captures soot from diesel exhaust to reduce emissions. When it becomes restricted or blocked you may notice excessive smoke, a warning light on the dash, reduced power or increased fuel consumption. We diagnose the root cause of DPF issues and carry out the appropriate service or repair to get your engine running cleanly again.
The Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) valve is an emissions component that recirculates a portion of exhaust gas back into the intake. EGR valves are notoriously troublesome on diesel 4WDs — they can stick, clog or fail, causing poor engine performance, rough running and warning lights. We diagnose EGR faults properly, find the root cause, and carry out the right repair rather than just clearing the code.
A well-executed ECU remap can improve fuel economy, particularly for vehicles used for long highway runs or consistent towing. By optimising the fuelling and ignition maps, the engine works more efficiently. We will tell you honestly whether your driving profile is likely to see fuel economy gains.
We work on all major 4WD brands including Toyota, Nissan, Ford, Mitsubishi, Isuzu, Mazda and Jeep. If you are unsure whether we can help with your specific vehicle, give us a call and we will confirm before you book.
